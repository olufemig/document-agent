# Media Rights Metadata Platform

```yaml
title: Media Rights Metadata Platform
sector: Media and Entertainment
client_type: Streaming media provider
capabilities:
  - Metadata management
  - Digital asset management
  - Workflow design
problem: Rights, content and distribution metadata were maintained in separate tools, creating risk when content was scheduled across markets.
solution: Built a governed metadata platform and approval workflow for rights-aware content operations.
technologies:
  - Contentful
  - Neo4j
  - Node.js
  - AWS Lambda
  - Amazon OpenSearch
outcomes:
  - Clearer view of rights restrictions
  - More reliable content scheduling information
  - Improved metadata ownership
lessons:
  - Rights data needs explicit lifecycle management
  - Metadata quality must be built into editorial workflows
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Content operations staff had to consult several systems to confirm whether a programme could be distributed in a particular market and period. Inconsistent metadata created avoidable scheduling risk.

We created a connected metadata model for titles, rights windows and territories, then introduced approval steps for key changes. Search and operational views were designed for editorial and distribution teams rather than technical users alone.

The platform gave teams a clearer view of rights constraints before scheduling decisions were finalised. The central lesson was that metadata quality improves when ownership is part of everyday editorial work.
