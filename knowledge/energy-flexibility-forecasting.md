```yaml

  - Forecasting

  - Google Cloud
  - Pub/Sub
  - Dataflow
  - BigQuery
  - Vertex AI
outcomes:
  - Better visibility of local demand patterns
  - More informed flexibility planning
  - Faster analysis of network constraints
lessons:
  - Forecasts need weather and local-event context
  - Network planners need uncertainty presented clearly
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

The operator received smart-meter and network data at different frequencies, making it difficult to create a common view of emerging demand constraints.

We built streaming and batch pipelines that brought the priority feeds together. Forecasts were presented alongside confidence ranges and the operational factors most likely to affect local demand.

Network planners gained a clearer basis for discussing flexibility options before constraints became urgent. The programme reinforced that uncertainty needs to be visible, not hidden behind a single forecast value.
