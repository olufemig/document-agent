# Student Support Insight Service

```yaml
title: Student Support Insight Service
sector: Higher Education
client_type: University
capabilities:
  - Student analytics
  - Data governance
  - Service design
problem: Student support teams could not combine attendance, engagement and case information in time to offer proactive help.
solution: Designed a governed insight service that highlighted students needing a supportive outreach conversation.
technologies:
  - Snowflake
  - dbt
  - Tableau
  - Microsoft Entra ID
outcomes:
  - Earlier identification of support needs
  - More coordinated outreach across services
  - Clearer controls for sensitive student data
lessons:
  - Insights must support professional judgement, not automate decisions
  - Data access should reflect the sensitivity of each support context
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Student services held useful information across separate systems, but staff typically learned about difficulties only after a student had already disengaged.

We worked with student-support and data-protection teams to define appropriate indicators, access rules and intervention pathways. The service gave authorised advisers a concise view of relevant signals while retaining staff judgement over every contact.

The university could coordinate support earlier without treating analytics as an automated decision-maker. The main lesson was that safeguards and service design need to develop alongside the data product.
