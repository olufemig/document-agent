All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

The bank's fraud controls were effective at identifying suspicious activity but produced a large number of cases requiring manual review. This created pressure on investigation teams.

We developed a supervised learning model using historical case outcomes and transaction behaviour. Rather than replacing existing rules, the model provided an additional risk score to support prioritisation.

The approach reduced noise and helped investigators focus on more credible cases. The key lesson was that explainability and traceability were as important as predictive performance.
