All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Project teams delivered drawings, models and asset records in a range of formats. Operations teams often had to rework information before they could use it for maintenance and planning.

We agreed a practical information standard with project and operations stakeholders, then introduced checks for required fields and document status. A shared repository gave teams a clearer view of handover readiness.

The approach helped surface information gaps earlier in delivery and made expectations clearer for suppliers. It reinforced that digital handover starts with operational requirements, not software configuration.
