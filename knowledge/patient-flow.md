```yaml

  - Python

  - Databricks
  - Power BI
outcomes:
  - Earlier visibility of likely capacity pressure
  - Better planning of beds and staffing
  - Improved operational decision support
lessons:
  - Operational adoption matters as much as model accuracy
  - Predictions should be presented with clear confidence ranges
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

The hospital relied heavily on historical averages and manual judgement when planning capacity. This made it difficult to respond early to changes in admission patterns.

We created a forecasting pipeline using historical admissions, discharge patterns, seasonal factors and operational indicators. Predictions were surfaced through a simple dashboard designed with bed management teams.

The solution helped planners identify likely pressure earlier and supported more proactive decisions. The main lesson was that a slightly simpler model with clear explanations was more useful than a more complex model that users struggled to interpret.
