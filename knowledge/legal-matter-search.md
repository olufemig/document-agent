# 9. Legal Matter Knowledge Search

```yaml
title: Legal Matter Knowledge Search
sector: Legal Services
client_type: Commercial law firm
capabilities:
  - Generative AI
  - Retrieval augmented generation
  - Knowledge management
problem: Lawyers found it difficult to locate relevant precedent and prior matter experience.
solution: Developed a secure RAG-based search assistant across approved internal documents.
technologies:
  - Python
  - Gemini
  - Vertex AI
  - Vector database
  - Google Cloud
outcomes:
  - Faster retrieval of relevant precedent
  - Reduced time spent searching document repositories
  - Improved reuse of internal knowledge
lessons:
  - Access controls must follow source permissions
  - Responses should always show supporting references
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

The firm's knowledge base contained years of valuable material, but finding relevant precedent often depended on individual experience or manual search.

We implemented a retrieval-augmented generation service that searched approved matter documents and returned concise answers with supporting references. Existing permissions were preserved.

Lawyers were able to identify relevant material faster while retaining control over legal judgement. The key lesson was that citation and permission handling were fundamental to adoption.
