All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

The organisation was experimenting with AI across several teams, but review processes varied and responsibilities were unclear.

We developed a governance model covering use case intake, risk classification, approval and ongoing monitoring. The framework was aligned to existing architecture, data protection and security processes.

This created a consistent route from experimentation to controlled deployment. The central lesson was that governance should guide delivery rather than create a separate approval layer.
