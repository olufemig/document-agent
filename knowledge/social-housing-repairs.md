All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Repairs teams could track individual jobs but struggled to see recurring issues across properties and contractors. Information on completion quality and follow-up work was inconsistent.

We established shared status definitions, combined contractor updates with housing records, and introduced an exception workflow for repeat or overdue cases. Service teams helped design the measures used in the new reporting.

The provider could identify cases needing attention sooner and discuss contractor performance using more consistent information. The work reinforced the importance of measuring resident outcomes as well as job completion.
