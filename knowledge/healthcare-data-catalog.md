All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

The organisation had grown its reporting estate over several years without a consistent approach to metadata, ownership or business definitions. Teams often recreated datasets because they could not easily find or trust existing assets.

We designed a data catalogue operating model, defined ownership and stewardship roles, and prioritised high-value domains for onboarding. The technical implementation was supported by workshops with clinical, operational and analytical teams.

The programme gave users a clearer view of available data, improved confidence in reporting and created a stronger foundation for wider governance. A key lesson was that technology alone does not create trust; ownership and decision rights need to be explicit.
