  - Data quality rules should be agreed with operational owners

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

The department managed multiple grant schemes using different systems and processes. This made it difficult to create a consistent view of recipients, payments and programme performance.

We developed a cloud-based integration layer and introduced common data structures across priority schemes. Automated quality checks were added to identify missing or inconsistent records.

The result was a more reliable view of grant activity and a simpler reporting process. A recurring lesson was that technical integration only works well when operational teams agree on common definitions.
