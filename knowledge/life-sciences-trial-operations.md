# Clinical Trial Operations Data Hub

```yaml
title: Clinical Trial Operations Data Hub
sector: Life Sciences
client_type: Pharmaceutical research organisation
capabilities:
  - Clinical data integration
  - Data quality
  - Operational analytics
problem: Trial operations teams used separate systems for site activity, enrolment, monitoring and issue management.
solution: Created a governed data hub and operational dashboards for trial delivery teams.
technologies:
  - Databricks
  - Delta Lake
  - Azure Data Factory
  - Power BI
  - Great Expectations
outcomes:
  - More consistent view of trial progress
  - Earlier visibility of data quality issues
  - Better coordination between study teams
lessons:
  - Data checks need clear escalation routes
  - Operational metrics should be defined with study teams
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Study teams had to reconcile operational reports from several specialist systems before they could understand recruitment, monitoring activity and outstanding issues.

We integrated priority operational datasets into a governed hub and developed dashboards with study teams. Automated checks highlighted incomplete or inconsistent records and directed issues to the appropriate owners.

Teams gained a more consistent view of delivery while retaining established clinical oversight processes. The programme showed that data-quality controls need clear ownership if they are to drive timely action.
