All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Performance teams could see where delays occurred, but not always why certain problems repeated. Existing reports provided useful summaries but limited diagnostic insight.

We combined timetable, incident, rolling stock and historical delay data to create route-level and service-level analysis. Machine learning was used to identify combinations of factors associated with elevated delay risk.

The work gave operational teams a clearer basis for prioritising interventions. It also reinforced the importance of consistent incident coding, since weak source data quickly limited the value of more advanced analysis.
