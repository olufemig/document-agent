```yaml
  - Tableau
outcomes:
  - Improved customer segmentation
  - Faster access to behavioural insight
  - Reduced duplication across analytical teams
lessons:
  - Identity resolution should be treated as a core design problem
  - Customer data models need strong governance from the outset
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Different banking products maintained separate customer views, making it difficult to understand behaviour across channels. Analysts frequently created their own joins and interpretations.

We established a governed customer model and integrated product, transaction and digital interaction data. Standard transformations and shared definitions reduced inconsistency between teams.

The platform improved segmentation and supported more relevant customer engagement. The main lesson was that identity resolution and common definitions need early agreement if the platform is to remain trusted.
