# Charity Donor Services Platform

```yaml
title: Charity Donor Services Platform
sector: Nonprofit
client_type: National charity
capabilities:
  - Customer relationship management
  - Data integration
  - Supporter insight
problem: Donor and supporter information was fragmented across different fundraising, events and contact-centre systems.
solution: Implemented a unified supporter profile and service workflows for fundraising and supporter-care teams.
technologies:
  - Salesforce Data Cloud
  - MuleSoft
  - Salesforce Service Cloud
  - Tableau
outcomes:
  - More complete view of supporter interactions
  - Improved coordination between fundraising and care teams
  - More consistent consent handling
lessons:
  - Consent preferences must be visible at the point of contact
  - Service processes should be designed around supporter needs
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

The charity's teams had separate records of donations, event participation and supporter enquiries. This made it difficult to provide a consistent service or understand a supporter's history.

We connected priority systems to create a governed supporter profile and redesigned key service workflows. Consent and communication preferences were made visible to staff during supporter interactions.

The charity gained a clearer view of relationships across its teams and reduced avoidable handoffs. The work highlighted that donor technology should improve supporter care, not just campaign reporting.
