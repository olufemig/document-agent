# 8. Government Policy Insight Platform

```yaml
title: Government Policy Insight Platform
sector: Government
client_type: Public sector policy organisation
capabilities:
  - Analytics
  - Natural language processing
  - Data visualisation
problem: Analysts spent significant time reviewing large volumes of consultation responses.
solution: Introduced NLP-assisted thematic analysis to support policy teams.
technologies:
  - Python
  - Gemini
  - BigQuery
  - Looker
outcomes:
  - Faster thematic analysis
  - Improved consistency of initial categorisation
  - More analyst time available for interpretation
lessons:
  - Human review remains essential for policy-sensitive analysis
  - AI outputs should be traceable to source material
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Policy teams were processing thousands of free-text consultation responses manually. The approach was thorough but slow and difficult to scale.

We built an NLP workflow that grouped responses into themes, highlighted recurring concerns and linked summaries back to original submissions. Analysts remained responsible for final interpretation.

The platform accelerated the first stage of analysis while preserving human oversight. The most important lesson was that transparency and source traceability were essential for trust.
