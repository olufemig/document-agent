```yaml
outcomes:
  - Faster first-pass review

  - Reduced administrative effort
lessons:
  - AI recommendations should remain advisory
  - Clause standards need regular maintenance
```

All content in this case study is fictional demonstration content.

Legal teams spent substantial time checking recurring contract provisions and comparing them against standard positions.

We created an assistant that extracted key clauses, compared them with approved templates and highlighted areas requiring attention. Lawyers could then focus on interpretation and negotiation.

The solution reduced repetitive work without removing professional oversight. A key lesson was that maintaining high-quality clause standards was critical to long-term performance.
